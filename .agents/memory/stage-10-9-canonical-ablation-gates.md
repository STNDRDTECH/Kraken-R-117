name: Stage 10.9 canonical ablation gates
description: Metastability comparisons may alter measurements but must inherit full-baseline authority eligibility.
---

Counterfactual Stage 10.9 arms must derive rollback and adaptive-credit
eligibility from a sidecar replay that exactly follows the full baseline's raw
ticks and experiment-level route selection. Every authority-bearing event
withheld by that baseline must be withheld before an ablated arm evaluates its
own topology or learning state.

**Why:** A local counterfactual can otherwise turn a baseline-stale,
non-creditable, or physiologically inhibited record into apparent adaptive
credit after an ablation changes topology or pressure.

**How to apply:** Keep the public reducer control-free. Any private comparison
path may change descriptive counterforces only after it receives the canonical
baseline's inhibition and withheld-event decisions; never treat ablated state as
the authority source.
