Operational evidence may shape only bounded read-only advisory cognition. Durable route, tactic, and connection changes require independently verified grounded execution.

**Why:** Allowing operational observations into durable reducers creates an authority bypass even when the surrounding settlement is structurally valid.

**How to apply:** Keep the public plastic-routing and adaptive chokepoints aligned; non-grounded evidence must leave durable state unchanged.

Invalidating an older update must require an explicit provenance-bound causal parent plus continuity of the independently executed test criterion, then deterministically replay only the later valid suffix on the current live branch.

**Why:** A signed caller-supplied parent or target envelope proves tamper binding, not causality; an unrelated failure could otherwise revoke valid learning. Retained audits alone also cannot distinguish a rolled-back successor from a live one.

**How to apply:** Bind the verifier record to the exact action and test contract that originally earned credit, require the later grounded failure to use that same criterion, preserve consumed identities, and replay only live successors in order.

Connection lifecycle proof must survive general audit-window eviction. Recovery remains possible after weakening or dormancy, while retirement is permitted only from proven dormancy at the weight floor and is terminal.

**Why:** Endpoint-only lifecycle checks permit forged transitions, and coupling formation proof to a rolling audit window creates a hidden lifetime cap.

**How to apply:** Retain a separately bounded grounded lifecycle ledger and replay its operation and weight progression when validating adaptive state.

Grounded delivery identity tombstones must outlive expiring receipt metadata and remain bounded and durable across restarts.

**Why:** Deleting an expired receipt must not make the same grounded execution claimable again, while unbounded durable receipts create a different availability failure.

**How to apply:** Migrate durable ledger schemas fail-closed, retain non-expiring claimed identities under a fixed capacity, expire only metadata, and reject malformed or exhausted state explicitly.
