Operational evidence may shape only bounded read-only advisory cognition. Durable route, tactic, and connection changes require independently verified grounded execution.

**Why:** Allowing operational observations into durable reducers creates an authority bypass even when the surrounding settlement is structurally valid.

**How to apply:** Keep the public plastic-routing and adaptive chokepoints aligned; non-grounded evidence must leave durable state unchanged.

Invalidating an older update must require an explicit provenance-bound causal parent and deterministically replay the later valid suffix from a trusted pre-update checkpoint.

**Why:** Restoring a checkpoint wholesale erases valid learning that happened after the bad update; subtracting the old delta is also wrong at saturation bounds.

**How to apply:** Preserve consumed identities, remove only the causally targeted update, and recompute later bounded effects in order.

Connection lifecycle proof must survive general audit-window eviction. Recovery remains possible after weakening or dormancy, while retirement is permitted only from proven dormancy at the weight floor and is terminal.

**Why:** Endpoint-only lifecycle checks permit forged transitions, and coupling formation proof to a rolling audit window creates a hidden lifetime cap.

**How to apply:** Retain a separately bounded grounded lifecycle ledger and replay its operation and weight progression when validating adaptive state.
