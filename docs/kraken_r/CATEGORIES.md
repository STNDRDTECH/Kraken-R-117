# Kraken-R category boundaries

The registry is a living archaeology record, not an import graph or a claim
that every named mechanism is reachable. Current status and evidence are
separate from intended role.

## 16 census areas

1. **Core execution** — current daemon and autonomous-cycle spine; remains
   legacy reference authority.
2. **Task state and reasoning** — task lifecycle, hypotheses, plans, and
   reasoning stacks; adapt the strongest evidence-backed state rather than
   creating another state authority.
3. **Evidence and settlement** — receipts, decision records, and outcome
   comparisons; reconcile existing ledgers before any future consolidation.
4. **Memory and learning** — primitives, retrieval, compression, and learning
   updates; a stored pattern is not a causal success.
5. **Emergence and routing** — signals, pressure, pathways, topology, and
   cascade routing; existing EventBus implementations remain classified.
6. **Homeostasis and health** — health controllers, stagnation, and phase
   observation; no new controller is allowed in this phase.
7. **Governance and safety** — directives, invariants, Angel, Nemesis, and the
   proposal bridge; safety boundaries precede adaptation.
8. **Evolution and mutation** — Kinetic, authority, lineage, organ, and
   regression mechanisms; mutations remain explicit proposals.
9. **Specialized processes** — HOP, workers, watchdogs, dormant daemons, and
   colony concepts; helpers do not prove a live daemon colony.
10. **J4H semantic review** — outbound semantic reporting is evidenced; an
    inbound judge is not assumed without evidence.
11. **Domain adapters** — corpus, SWEBench, real-repository, and synthesis
    providers; adapters must not define the shared vocabulary.
12. **Tools and execution** — sandbox, Orzhaal Bubble, refinery, and routers;
    preserve existing execution boundaries.
13. **Operator surfaces** — dashboard, training, and inspection commands;
    operator visibility is not runtime authority.
14. **Alternate and legacy stacks** — recursive stacks and dormant hubs;
    preserve them for later salvage and comparison.
15. **Design assets** — specifications, architecture notes, and attached
    fossils; declared intent is not reachability.
16. **Distinctive concepts** — sovereignty, ground-truth final judgment, and
    future research mechanisms; each must carry evidence and authority labels.

## Planned mechanisms

The following remain design-only and non-authoritative:

- metaplasticity;
- neuromodulation;
- reservoir-style dynamics;
- criticality and phase control;
- organizational engrams;
- offline consolidation;
- causal lesion and shadow experiments;
- developmental specialization and daemon specialization;
- hyperdimensional associative state;
- hierarchical learning levels.

Future work may refine one of these only as a bounded change with a registry
entry, explicit evidence, a regression condition, and no silent replacement of
legacy ROGAL.
